Why one ticket held three

Decision 0001 merges on the subject line alone, so nobody has to read the body. Every report here has a subject like "Date is wrong" or "Wrong day on the invite". Twelve subjects cover all 412, and they describe the symptom a person noticed, never the bug underneath.

The decision names this consequence and accepts it: "Reports with different subjects about the same bug end up in separate tickets." The ticket shows the opposite too, and that one is not written down: reports with the same subject about different bugs end up in one ticket, and the count makes it look settled.

A count is evidence of how many people are affected. It is not evidence of how many things are broken.
